Privacy Policy

How Manish Steel Traders Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses and protects your information — including how we communicate with you over WhatsApp.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Who we are

This website, manishsteeltraders.com, is operated by Manish Steel Traders Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our"), a steel distributor and stockist based at 17 Chandak Layout, Ghat Road, Ganeshpeth Colony, Nagpur, Maharashtra 440018, India (GSTIN 27AANCM8559N1ZE). We are the party responsible for your personal data described in this policy. If you have any questions, contact us at info@manishsteeltraders.com or +91 98222 21699.

2. What information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, or that is needed to serve your enquiry:

  • Contact detailsYour name, phone / WhatsApp number, email address and business or site location.
  • Enquiry detailsThe products, sizes, grades, quantities and delivery information you send us when you request a quote.
  • MessagesThe content of the calls, WhatsApp messages and emails you exchange with us.
  • Website usageBasic, non-identifying technical information (such as pages viewed) that your browser sends when you visit this site.

We do not ask for, and do not knowingly collect, sensitive personal data such as government ID numbers, bank details or passwords through this website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • RespondReply to your enquiries and prepare and send price quotes.
  • Serve ordersCoordinate quotations, orders, delivery and payment.
  • CommunicateSend you rate lists, order updates and, where you have agreed to receive them, occasional offers relevant to steel supply.
  • ImproveUnderstand which products customers ask about so we can keep the right stock and improve our service.

Our lawful basis for processing is your consent and the legitimate need to respond to and fulfil your business enquiry.

4. WhatsApp communication

We communicate with customers over WhatsApp using the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which is provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you message us on WhatsApp, or when you give us your number and agree to be contacted:

  • How it is sentYour messages are delivered through WhatsApp and are also processed by Meta under WhatsApp's own terms. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Our messaging partnerWe use an authorised messaging provider (a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider) to send and manage these messages on our behalf. They process your number and message content only to deliver our communications.
  • ConsentWe send WhatsApp messages only to people who have contacted us, given us their number, or otherwise agreed to be contacted. We do not send messages to numbers obtained without a legitimate basis.
  • Opting outYou can stop receiving marketing or promotional WhatsApp messages from us at any time by replying STOP, or by messaging or calling us on +91 98222 21699. We will act on your request promptly.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providersWhatsApp / Meta and our authorised messaging provider, so that we can communicate with you; and other trusted providers who help us run our business (for example transport for deliveries), only as needed.
  • LegalAuthorities or advisors where we are required to by law, or to protect our rights.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry and contact details only for as long as needed to serve you and to maintain our ordinary business, accounting and tax records, after which we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your details sooner (see section 8).

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and limit access to it to the people who need it to serve you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • AccessTell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • CorrectUpdate information that is wrong or out of date.
  • DeleteRemove your information from our records.
  • Stop contactStop sending you marketing messages.

To make any of these requests, email info@manishsteeltraders.com or call +91 98222 21699. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Website & cookies

This is a simple informational website. It does not run its own login, shopping cart or advertising trackers. The enquiry form on our Contact page does not send data to a server — it simply opens WhatsApp on your device with your message ready to send. Some embedded features (such as the Google Maps location) may set their own cookies, governed by those providers' privacy policies.

10. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, with the "Last updated" date at the top showing when it last changed.
