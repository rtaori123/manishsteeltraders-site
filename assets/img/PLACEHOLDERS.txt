IMAGE ASSETS — what to drop in here
====================================

The site currently uses:
  logo.svg      — placeholder logo (a steel bolt-head badge + I-beam mark)
  favicon.svg   — placeholder browser-tab icon

Replace / add real images (keep the same filenames to avoid editing HTML):

  logo.svg                 -> your real logo (SVG preferred; or logo.png ~200x200)
  favicon.svg              -> your icon (or add favicon.png / favicon.ico)
  tmt-bars.jpg             -> photo of TMT bars at your yard        (products page)
  structural-steel.jpg     -> photo of angles/channels/beams        (products page)
  yard-or-team.jpg         -> yard / team / founder photo           (about page)
  service-area.jpg         -> map or delivery-area image            (about page)

Where the photo placeholders are: search the HTML for the class "ph" (the
striped grey boxes). Replace a placeholder like:

    <div class="ph"><span>Photo: TMT bars ...</span></div>

with a real image:

    <img src="assets/img/tmt-bars.jpg" alt="TMT reinforcement bars at Manish Steel Traders, Nagpur">

Tips: use JPGs ~1200px wide, compressed (TinyPNG etc.) so the site stays fast
on mobile data. Always fill the alt="" text (good for SEO + accessibility).
