IMAGE ASSETS — what to drop in here
====================================

Already real:
  logo-mark.png   — the gold "M" emblem (header + footer badge)
  logo-full.png   — full logo with the name (available if you want it on a light area)
  favicon.png     — browser-tab icon
  (logo.svg / favicon.svg are the old placeholders, now unused — safe to delete)

Photos to add — each is currently a light grey "photo" box on the page. Replace the
box with an <img> (keep these filenames and it stays tidy):

  hero-steel.jpg        -> Home hero (steel yard / stock)
  tmt-bars.jpg          -> Products, TMT section
  structural-steel.jpg  -> Products, structural section
  about-yard.jpg        -> About, "Our story"
  director.jpg          -> About, "Leadership"  ***REAL photo of Manish Taori***

How to swap a placeholder: find a box like
    <div class="photo photo--tall"><span>Photo — ...</span></div>
and replace it with
    <img class="photo photo--tall" src="assets/img/director.jpg"
         alt="Manish Taori, Director, Manish Steel Traders">
(For a normal 4:3 slot use class="photo" without --tall. Keep the alt text.)

DIRECTOR PHOTO: must be a genuine photo of Manish Taori — do not use a stock photo
of someone else. A plain, well-lit headshot (or him at the yard) works best.

Licensing: only use photos you own or that are cleared for commercial use
(your own photos are ideal). For the map on Contact, paste a Google Maps embed
(see README), not an image.

Tips: JPGs ~1200px wide, compressed (TinyPNG) so the site stays fast on mobile.
