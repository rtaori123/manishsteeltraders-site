How to swap a placeholder: find a box like
    <div class="photo photo--tall"><span>Photo — ...</span></div>
and replace it with
    <img class="photo photo--tall" src="assets/img/director.jpg"
         alt="Manish Taori, Director, Manish Steel Traders">
(For a normal 4:3 slot use class="photo" without --tall. Keep the alt text.)

DIRECTOR PHOTO: must be a genuine photo of Manish Taori — do not use a stock photo
of someone else. A plain, well-lit headshot (or him at the yard) works best.

BRAND LOGOS — for the brand cards on quality.html#brands
=======================================================
ALL FIVE ARE IN PLACE — on the home page logo row and the quality.html brand cards:
  brand-tiger.png     -> SEL Tiger TMT
  brand-kamdhenu.webp -> Kamdhenu
  brand-sangam.webp   -> Sangam Steel
  brand-sky.png       -> Sky TMT  (skyalloys.co.in — NOT YET SAVED)
  brand-lodha.jpg     -> Lodha Gold TMT

These came off the manufacturers' own websites, so they have white backgrounds
rather than transparency (fine — the boxes they sit in are white too) and are
fairly small files. If you can get the dealer logo pack from Sangam or Kamdhenu,
their versions will be sharper: Sangam's carries an "A PRODUCT OF ofbusiness"
banner, and Kamdhenu's is the corporate group mark rather than the TMT product
mark. Save any replacement under the SAME filename and it swaps automatically.

Transparent PNG or SVG, around 340x144 or larger. To swap, replace
    <div class="logo-ph">SEL Tiger TMT<br>logo</div>
with
    <img class="brand-logo" src="assets/img/brand-tiger.png" alt="SEL Tiger TMT logo">

Ask the manufacturer or your dealer contact for the logo pack rather than saving
images off their website — you get a clean transparent file and you can say where
it came from. We are not appointed dealers for these brands, so keep the wording
around any logo as "brands we supply" — never "authorised dealer".

Licensing: only use photos you own or that are cleared for commercial use
(your own photos are ideal). For the map on Contact, paste a Google Maps embed
(see README), not an image.

Tips: JPGs ~1200px wide, compressed (TinyPNG) so the site stays fast on mobile.
