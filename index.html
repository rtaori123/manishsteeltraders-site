Why we can quote keener

Bought direct from the mills

We buy our TMT direct from mills in Raipur, Raigarh and across Chhattisgarh — the belt that supplies much of central India's reinforcement steel. Buying at the source puts one less margin between the mill and your site, and that is the difference you see in the quote.

It also means we aren't waiting on somebody else's stock. Material sits in our own yard in Nagpur, so when you order we are loading, not sourcing.

  • Direct mill sourcing — Raipur, Raigarh and Chhattisgarh
  • Same-day delivery in Nagpur — order in the morning, steel on site the same day
  • Held in our own yard — 8 mm to 40 mm in the sizes that move
  • Mill test certificates — supplied on request, just ask
Who we are

Steel distributors, stockists and retailers

Manish Steel Traders Pvt. Ltd. is a family-run business at 17 Chandak Layout, Ghat Road, Nagpur. Since 1998 we have distributed, stocked and retailed TMT reinforcement bars and structural steel across Nagpur and the wider Vidarbha region.

Because we stock rather than only broker, we can quote quickly and supply without the wait. The business is led today by Manish Taori, Director, carrying forward the values that built our name.

Common questions

  • How quickly can you deliver? Within Nagpur, the same day. Elsewhere in Vidarbha, tell us the site and we'll confirm when we quote.
  • Which grades do you stock? Fe 500, Fe 500D and Fe 550D to IS 1786, in 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 25, 32 and 40 mm.
  • Will I get a test certificate? Yes — mill test certificates on request. Ask when you place the order.
  • I already have a supplier. Worth calling? Yes. Most people who call us are already buying somewhere. A quote costs nothing and there's no obligation.
  • Which brand should I take? Depends on the drawing and the budget. Tell us the project and we'll set out the options rather than push one.
  • Do you supply anything besides TMT? Channels, angles, rods, pipes, plates and binding wire — and we'll source most other steel on request.
