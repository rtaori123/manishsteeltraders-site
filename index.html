Who we are

A trusted name in steel for over 30 years

Manish Steel Traders Pvt. Ltd. is a family-run steel supplier based on Ghat Road, Nagpur. For three decades we have supplied TMT reinforcement bars and structural steel to the builders and developers of Nagpur and the Vidarbha region.

Our specialty is TMT bars — but what customers value most is dependable supply, honest pricing, and relationships that last.
