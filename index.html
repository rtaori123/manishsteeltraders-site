Who we are

Steel distributors, stockists and retailers

Manish Steel Traders Pvt. Ltd. is a family-run business on Ghat Road, Nagpur. Since 1998 we have distributed, stocked and retailed TMT reinforcement bars and structural steel across Nagpur and the wider Vidarbha region.

Because we stock rather than only broker, we can quote quickly and supply without the wait. The business is led today by Manish Taori, Director, carrying forward the values that built our name.
