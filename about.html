Our story

A Nagpur name in steel for 30+ years

From our base on Ghat Road, Nagpur, we've spent three decades supplying TMT reinforcement bars and structural steel to the renowned builders and developers of Nagpur and the wider Vidarbha region.

What started as a local steel trading business has stayed true to the same simple promise: dependable supply, honest pricing and material you can build on — order after order, project after project.

Today the business is led by Manish Taori, Director, carrying forward the same values of quality and trust that built our name.
