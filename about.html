About us

A family-run steel distributor, stockist and retailer in Nagpur — supplying the Vidarbha region since 1998.

Our story

Started in 1998, family-run ever since

Manish Steel Traders began in 1998 and has stayed what it started as — a family-run business, based on Ghat Road, Nagpur.

We are steel distributors, stockists and retailers. From here we have supplied the Vidarbha region for over 25 years, serving mainly the Nagpur metropolitan area: developers, builders, hardware retailers, contractors and engineers.

The promise has stayed the same throughout: dependable supply, honest pricing, and material you can build on — order after order. We are keen to keep expanding further across the region.

The business is led today by Manish Taori, Director, carrying forward the values of quality and trust that built our name.
