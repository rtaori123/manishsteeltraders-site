About us

A Nagpur steel supplier trusted by builders and developers for over 30 years.

Our story

Three decades of steel, built on trust

From our base on Ghat Road, Nagpur, Manish Steel Traders has spent over 30 years supplying TMT reinforcement bars and structural steel to the builders and developers of Nagpur and the wider Vidarbha region.

What began as a local steel trading business has kept the same simple promise throughout: dependable supply, honest pricing, and material you can build on — order after order.

The business is led today by Manish Taori, Director, carrying forward the values of quality and trust that built our name.
