Products

The steel we supply for construction across Nagpur and Vidarbha are TMT reinforcement bars and binding wire from our own yard, with structural sections and the wider range sourced to order and delivered to site.

Our Speciality

TMT reinforcement bars and binding wire are what we keep ready to load in our own yard. These are the two lines we supply day in, day out, delivered same day within Nagpur.

TMT reinforcement bars

Thermo-mechanically treated bars for slabs, columns, beams and footings — the core of what we do. We keep the full range in the yard, in the sizes and grades your design calls for (8–32 mm; Fe 500, Fe 500D and Fe 550D to IS 1786), each quality-checked before dispatch. Not sure which grade or how much you need? Send your requirement and we'll confirm what fits.

Working out quantities? Our TMT weight chart gives kg per metre, per bar and bars per tonne.

Call for rates WhatsApp a requirement

Binding wire

Soft-annealed steel wire for tying reinforcement steady before the pour. We keep it in the yard alongside the bars, so it loads onto the same delivery — no second trip and no chasing it down separately. Tell us the quantity and we'll supply the gauge to suit.
