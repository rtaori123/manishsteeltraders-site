Our specialty

TMT reinforcement bars

High-strength TMT (thermo-mechanically treated) bars for slabs, columns, beams and footings — the core of what we supply. We keep the full range in stock, in the grades and brands your design demands.

  • Sizes — 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 25, 32 and 40 mm
  • Grades — Fe500, Fe500D and Fe550D (conforming to IS 1786, ISI-marked)
  • Leading brands — reputed national & regional TMT brands trusted in the region
  • Applications — RCC slabs, columns, beams and footings; residential to commercial
  • Quality-checked — correct grade and size, verified before dispatch
