Quality you can build on

Structures depend on the steel inside them — above all, the TMT bars in your columns and slabs. We keep quality simple and dependable: right grade, correct size, every order.

Our commitment

Right material, right grade, every time

Since 1998, builders, contractors and retailers have kept coming back because the steel we supply performs — batch after batch. We source from established manufacturers and check what leaves our yard, so what reaches your site is what you ordered.

If it isn't right, we make it right. That accountability is why relationships with us last decades.

What we assure

How we keep quality dependable

  • Genuine material, never duplicate — bought direct from the mill, so a branded bar is that brand. We don't deal in duplicate or look-alike material, whatever the margin on it.
  • Trusted sources — steel sourced from established, reputed manufacturers.
  • Correct grade & size — supplied exactly as ordered for your design.
  • Quality-checked stock — material inspected before it leaves the yard.
  • ISI-marked & IS 1786 — our branded TMT range conforms to the Bureau of Indian Standards (Fe500 / Fe500D / Fe550D). Ask us and we will confirm the marking and grade for the brand you are quoted.
  • Proper handling & storage — kept in good condition until delivery.
  • Stand-behind service — any issue, and we set it right.

Every TMT bar we supply is chosen for consistent quality — the right grade, the correct size, and the dependable performance your structure is built on.

The brands we supply

SEL Tiger TMT

The flagship reinforcement brand of Shyam Metalics, made by a fully integrated ore-to-metal route using German Thermex technology. Bars go through a three-stage quench, self-temper and atmospheric cooling cycle, giving a hard outer surface over a ductile core. Its "Tiger Bond" rib pattern is designed for grip in concrete.

Sourcing

Where our steel comes from

We buy direct from mills in Raipur, Raigarh and across Chhattisgarh — the belt that rolls much of central India's reinforcement steel. Buying at the source rather than through another trader is what lets us quote the way we do, and it means we know which mill a batch came off.

Material is then held in our own yard on Ghat Road, so what we quote is what we can actually deliver — not stock we still have to go and find. Within Nagpur that means same-day delivery to your site.

Before anything is dispatched we check the grade and the size against what you ordered, and mill test certificates come with the material on request. If something isn't right, we make it right.
