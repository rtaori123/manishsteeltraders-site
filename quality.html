The TMT brands we supply

National names and mills right here in Vidarbha, so we can match the brand to your project and your budget without dropping below the grade your design needs. Here is what each one is known for.

SEL Tiger TMT

The flagship reinforcement brand of Shyam Metalics, made by a fully integrated ore-to-metal route using German Thermex technology. Bars go through a three-stage quench, self-temper and atmospheric cooling cycle, giving a hard outer surface over a ductile core. Its "Tiger Bond" rib pattern is designed for grip in concrete.

Fe 500DFe 550DThermex

Visit the official SEL Tiger website ↗

Kamdhenu

One of the largest reinforcement bar brands in the country and among the most widely specified by name. BIS and ISO certified to IS 1786 across its grade range, with low sulphur and phosphorus content for weldability and ductility. If your consultant has written a brand into the drawing, it is often this one.

Fe 500Fe 500DFe 550Fe 550D

Visit the official Kamdhenu website ↗

Sangam Steel

Their Vidarbha regional mill is an integrated plant at Wardha, a short haul from Nagpur, and the leading reinforcement producer in the region. Runs the licensed Thermex process from H&K GmbH, Germany, is BIS (ISI) registered to IS 1786-2008, and holds ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Rolls the full range of common sizes, and being close by it replenishes quickly.

Fe 500Fe 550Fe 550DMade in Vidarbha

Visit the official Sangam Steel website ↗

Sky TMT

From Sky Alloys and Power Limited, an integrated producer at Raigarh in Chhattisgarh is one of the mills we buy from direct. Integrated means they run the whole chain themselves, from sponge iron through billets to the finished bar, so the steel is controlled from raw material rather than bought in part-made. Being on our sourcing route, it is also one of the lines we can replenish quickest.

Raigarh, ChhattisgarhIntegrated millDirect from source

Visit Sky Alloys & Power, the maker of Sky TMT ↗

Lodha Gold TMT

A local brand rolled at the Lodha plant on Umred Road, right here in Nagpur. Because we keep it in our own yard, we can supply it fast and in any quantity, from a few bundles for a repair to a full truck for a slab. For routine Nagpur work where the drawing doesn't name a national brand, it is often the practical pick.

NR TMT

Rolled in the Raigarh steel belt of Chhattisgarh and stocked in the common construction sizes. A practical option for keeping a project's steel budget in check on routine work. Tell us the size and quantity, and we will quote it against the alternatives so you can decide.

Raigarh, ChhattisgarhCommon sizes

What we assure

How we keep quality dependable

  • Genuine material, never duplicate. A branded bar is that brand, sourced from the mill. We do not deal in look-alike material, whatever the margin on it.
  • ISI-marked, IS 1786 grades. Our branded TMT conforms to the Bureau of Indian Standards (Fe500 / Fe500D / Fe550D). Ask and we will confirm the marking for the brand you are quoted.
  • Correct grade and size, every order. Supplied exactly as your design calls for, and checked before it leaves the yard.
  • Mill test certificates on request. The paperwork comes with the material whenever you need it.
  • Stand-behind service. If something is not right, we make it right. That is why accounts stay with us for decades.
Sourcing

Where our steel comes from

We source from established mills across the country's main reinforcement belts — Shyam Metalics in the east for SEL Tiger, the integrated mills around Raigarh in Chhattisgarh for brands like Sky and NR, and plants right here in Vidarbha such as Sangam at Wardha and Lodha in Nagpur. Buying close to the source keeps the chain short, and it means we know which mill a batch came off.

Material is then held in our own yard on Ghat Road, so what we quote is what we can actually deliver — not stock we still have to go and find. Within Nagpur that means same-day delivery to your site.

Before anything is dispatched we check the grade and the size against what you ordered, and mill test certificates come with the material on request. If something isn't right, we make it right.
